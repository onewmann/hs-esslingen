# Data

Two small files for tests and simulations and the four camera recordings in
`recordings/` come from the 2025 student project. `measurements/` is the
folder for measurement sessions of your own; it holds two synthetic example
sessions. The data files in this folder are under CC BY 4.0
([licence of the data](#licence-of-the-data)).

## `testsequences.mat`

Four synthetic sequences of 256 x 256 px, stored as `uint8` (divide by 255).
Each frame is the previous frame shifted down and to the right with zero fill,
so the black border grows from frame to frame. Analyse only the area that
holds image content in every frame of a window (see `tests/matlab/test_testsequences.m`).

| Variable | Frames | Motion per frame (x right, y down) |
|---|---|---|
| `model` | 20 | (5, 5) px, constant |
| `tyre` | 20 | (4, 4) px, constant |
| `vehicle` | 30 | about (5, 5) px, single steps with vx and vy between 1.7 and 7.8 px (3.50 to 9.93 px long) |
| `accelerating` | 30 | vy = 3 px; vx rises from 5.8 to 8 px, falls to 2 px and rises again to 5 px |

Converted from `Testsequenzen1.mat` of the 2025 student project (variables
`modelSequence`, `reifenSequence`, `fahrzeugSequence`, `simSequence`; values in
[0, 1] scaled to 0..255).

## `surface_texture.png`

Central 1024 x 1024 px of the first frame of recording `recordings/vid2.mat`
(see below). Used as a realistic texture for simulations:
`fftvel.simulate_pulsed_image` with the texture (MATLAB option `'Texture'`,
Python argument `texture=`) moves a fixed window over it with exact, known
shifts.

## `recordings/vid1.mat` to `recordings/vid4.mat`

Four recordings of a Basler camera (Mono8, 1936 x 1216 px), 10 frames each,
as `getdata` of the MATLAB Image Acquisition Toolbox returns them. Each file
holds one variable, `recording`: 1216 x 1936 x 1 x 10 `uint8` (rows x
columns x 1 colour channel x frames).

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `vid1.mat` | static room scene, no motion (the mean brightness rises from about 110 to 136 over the 10 frames) |
| `vid2.mat` | moving textured surface, steps of 2.9 to 8.2 px between frames |
| `vid3.mat` | moving textured surface, steps of 4.5 to 18.0 px between frames |
| `vid4.mat` | moving textured surface, steps of 1.6 to 6.0 px between frames |

The steps are the displacement between consecutive frames by phase
correlation of the central 1024 x 1024 px. They are not uniform, while the
method assumes equal steps between the frames it averages, so the estimator
rejects all six 9-frame windows of `vid2` to `vid4`; new recordings at a
fixed frame rate are in preparation.
`python experiments/run.py --only recordings` analyses this (the folder
defaults to `data/recordings`) and writes `experiments/results/recordings.csv`
and `docs/figures/recordings.png`.

`fftvel.pulsed_from_stack` (MATLAB and Python) and
`python -m fftvel estimate data/recordings/vid2.mat --stack --exposures 9`
read a recording as it is. With 10 frames, an even number, they need the
number of frames to average: `exposures` (MATLAB `'Exposures'`, command line
`--exposures`) or `L`.

## `measurements/`

One subfolder per measurement session, each with a `meta.csv` that
`experiments/real_measurements.py` evaluates. `synthetic_average/` (frame
bursts) and `synthetic_flash/` (multi-flash images) are simulated examples of
the format, written by the `make_example.py` script in each of them.
[`measurements/README.md`](measurements/README.md) describes the format.
